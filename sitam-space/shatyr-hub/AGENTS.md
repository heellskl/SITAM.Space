## SITAM SPACE v20 — account safety (override older notes above)
- Registration does not prove the e-mail (v9.5 auto-confirm), and neither does a personal e-mail. So `create_account_claims(user, e-mail)` runs at sign-up and when a personal e-mail is added (trigger `profiles_personal_email_claims`): a lab seat listed with that e-mail becomes an `account_claims` row (coordinator: `decide_account_claim`, `AccountClaimsPanel` on /admin and Manage → Admin); a researcher card (`professors.email` or private `professors.alt_emails`) becomes a v17 `researcher_profile_requests` row ("This is me", approved with `review_researcher_request`). Nothing is linked automatically any more.
- Coordinators are bound to an account (`coordinator_emails.user_id`, filled by trigger when the row is inserted for an existing account). Partners typing an existing company name get `company_members.status = 'pending'` (`decide_company_member` by coordinator or company owner, `CompanyRequestsPanel`); `is_company_member` counts only `active`.
- Personal e-mail sign-in: `profiles.personal_email` is unique across accounts and never another account's sign-in e-mail (`profiles_personal_email_guard`, `enforce_signup_email`). The login form falls back to `sign_in_email(e-mail, password)` (bcrypt check, 10 tries / 15 min per e-mail) and signs in with the returned SDU e-mail.
- `my_context()` adds `pending_claims` (lab names), `pending_companies` (→ `caps.pendingClaims`, `caps.pendingCompanies`, shown on Profile).
- Ask SITAM: keyword scopes use Unicode-aware word starts (`(?<![\p{L}\p{N}])`, flag `u`) — `\b` does not work for Cyrillic. History is cleaned on the server (`cleanHistory`); failed questions are refunded (`ai_refund_quota`). `ai-index` removes stale documents on every run; `supabase/schedule-ai-index.sql` schedules it every 5 minutes (pg_cron + pg_net + Vault secret).
- Dates: "today" for the site = `sitamDay()` in api.ts (Asia/Almaty), never `toISOString().slice(0, 10)`.
- professors.json `other_emails` → `professors.alt_emails` (build-seed). Papers without Scopus EID or DOI are matched by title.
- Database: existing (v19) → `supabase/update-to-v20.sql`; empty → `supabase/setup-new-database.sql`. Tests: `permissions_tests.sql`, then `v20_tests.sql`.
